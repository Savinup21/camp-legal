Camp — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Camp (“we”, “us”) is a strength-training planner for combat-sport athletes. This policy explains what the Camp iOS app collects, why, who processes it, and the choices you have. Contact: savinu083@gmail.com.

The short version

  • Camp works without an account. Everything you enter stays on your phone unless you sign in (with Apple or Google) to back it up.
  • No ads, no tracking, no data sold. We don't use the advertising identifier (IDFA) and we don't ask to track you.
  • You can delete your account and all of its data inside the app at any time (Settings → Delete account and data).

What we collect

We do not collect your name, phone number, address, contacts, location, or browsing history. The private “How did it go?” camp note never leaves your phone.

Who processes your data

  • Supabase (database and server functions, hosted in the United States) — stores your backup.
  • Apple — Sign in with Apple and in-app purchases.
  • Google — Sign in with Google, if you choose it (we request your email only).
  • RevenueCat — manages subscription and purchase status.
  • OpenAI — receives a meal photo only when you use the photo estimate, to return an estimate. Photos are sent through our server and are not stored by us.
  • USDA FoodData Central and Open Food Facts — receive food search words or a scanned barcode only, never anything that identifies you.

These providers process data on our behalf to run the features above. We don't sell or share your data for advertising.

How long we keep it

Your backup is kept until you delete it. Deleting your account (in the app) removes your training, check-in, nutrition and purchase records from our servers, disconnects Sign in with Apple or Google, and removes the link between your account and usage events. Data on your phone is erased at the same time. Apple keeps its own purchase records under Apple's policies.

Children

Camp is for people aged 13 and over. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 13; the app stops onboarding if you tell us you're under 13. If you're under 18, use Camp with the involvement of a parent, guardian or coach. If you believe a child under 13 has used Camp, contact us and we'll delete the data.

Your choices and rights

  • Use Camp without signing in — nothing leaves your phone except anonymous usage events and the requests described above when you use search, barcode or photo features.
  • Delete everything at any time in Settings → Delete account and data.
  • Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or US state laws), you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your data, and to object to processing. Email us and we'll respond within 30 days.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections. Each athlete's backup is only readable by that athlete's account. No system is perfectly secure, but we limit what we collect to reduce risk.

Health information

Camp gives general fitness guidance, not medical advice. Check-in and nutrition data are used only to plan your training and are never used for advertising.

Changes

If we change this policy we'll update the date above, and tell you in the app for significant changes.
